# Homepage slideshow — image sources and rights

All four sourced from Wikimedia Commons (2026-11-19), converted to black and white and
resized to a 1920px-wide web-optimized JPEG. Originals and conversion script kept outside
this repo (session scratchpad); this file is the durable record of where each one came
from and what its rights status actually is, read directly off its Commons file page —
none inferred.

**Why these four:** Adrian's brief, 2026-11-19 — "deliberately generic photos," "relevant to food production and working people eating," black and white, in the register of a full-bleed slideshow (the same hero treatment Misfits Market's homepage uses). Two frames from the same 1943 Stockton, CA shoot were kept deliberately (both show real detail — the "Bienvenidos los Trabajadores Mexicanos" welcome sash in frame 01 is a real, legible, on-brand detail) rather than treated as a duplicate.
