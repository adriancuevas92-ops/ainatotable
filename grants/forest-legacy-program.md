# Forest Legacy Program

*Federal, administered through the state · September 11, 2026*

## Why it's relevant

Land-protection funding (conservation easements or acquisition) — relevant to keeping land in forest use long-term, not to funding restoration work on land already held.

## How it actually works

State-administered through Hawaiʻi's Division of Forestry & Wildlife, which nominates projects into the federal program.

## The deadline lesson

A real, well-funded program can still be the wrong tool for the job — check what the money is actually for (buying/protecting land vs. doing restoration work on it) before treating a deadline as relevant to you.

## About this date

Real 2026 nomination deadline, shown for reference.

## Where to start
