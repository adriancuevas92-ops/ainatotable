# Hoʻōla ʻĀina (Green Fee), DLNR / Division of Forestry & Wildlife

*State (Hawaiʻi) · August 17, 2026*

## Why it's relevant

State-funded, specifically for community-based restoration of forests, watersheds, and wetlands — as close a mission match to food-forest and land-restoration work as any program tracked here.

## How it actually works

A full proposal (not just a letter of intent) submitted through the state's HANDS portal during an open RFP window — the window is not year-round.

## The deadline lesson

This program isn't listed on Grants.gov at all — it's state, not federal — so a federal-only search will miss it entirely. Worth checking DLNR's own announcements directly, not just Grants.gov.

## About this date

2026 cycle's full-proposal deadline already passed. Next cycle not yet announced.

## Where to start

dlnr.hawaii.gov/blog and hands.ehawaii.gov — watch for the next RFP announcement
