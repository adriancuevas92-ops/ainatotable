# Competitive State Wildlife Grant Program

*Federal, administered through the state · September 18, 2026*

## Why it's relevant

Funds native-species habitat work, but the money typically flows to the state wildlife agency first.

## How it actually works

Usually the state agency (in Hawaiʻi, DLNR) is the direct applicant; a community organization would most likely need to be a named project partner or subawardee under the state's own application, not a solo direct applicant.

## The deadline lesson

A program being 'open' on Grants.gov doesn't mean a small organization can apply to it directly — check who the actual eligible applicant is before assuming a posted deadline applies to you.

## About this date

Real 2026 deadline for the state's own application — confirm you can be named as a partner well before this.

## Where to start

Hawaiʻi DLNR — ask directly whether community partners can be named on the state's application
