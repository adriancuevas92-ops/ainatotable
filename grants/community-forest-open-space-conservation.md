# Community Forest and Open Space Conservation Program (USDA Forest Service)

*Federal · October 13, 2026*

## Why it's relevant

Relevant if the real need is acquiring new land to protect, not restoring land already held.

## How it actually works

Funds fee-simple acquisition of forest land by qualified nonprofits, tribes, or local governments. Applications route through the state's own State Forester, not directly to the federal agency.

## The deadline lesson

Two different real programs can share the same federal assistance-listing number (CFDA) — one national, general-purpose version, and a separate, geographically-restricted version funded under a different initiative. Read which specific funding notice you're looking at, not just the number.

## About this date

Real 2026 deadline for the national (non-Great-Lakes) version of this program.

## Where to start

Hawaiʻi Division of Forestry & Wildlife (state forester's office)
