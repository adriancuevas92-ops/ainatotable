# Partners for Fish and Wildlife Program (USFWS)

*Federal · no fixed deadline — contact first*

## Why it's relevant

Funds invasive-species control, native out-planting, and habitat restoration on private land — close to the core work of building a food forest that includes real ecological restoration, not just planting.

## How it actually works

Contact-first, not a cold written submission. The program's own terms require reaching out to local Partners for Fish and Wildlife staff before an application exists — a biologist works with you to shape the project first.

## The deadline lesson

A Grants.gov posting can show a submission deadline that only applies to applicants already invited into the process. There can be an earlier, separate cutoff for a first-time contact — always ask directly rather than trusting the posted date alone.

## About this date

No posted deadline applies until you've made contact — reach out any time.

## Where to start

Pacific Islands Fish and Wildlife Office — pifwo_admin@fws.gov, (808) 792-9400
