# USDA Urban & Community Forestry Challenge Cost Share Grant Program

*Federal · October 12, 2026*

## Why it's relevant

Sounds like a food-forest match by name alone — a real caution, not a real fit every year.

## How it actually works

Each annual cycle has its own stated funding priority, which can shift year to year. The 2026 cycle's actual focus is wood utilization (turning pruning and removal waste into lumber, biochar, and similar products) rather than ecosystem restoration or food-bearing tree planting.

## The deadline lesson

A program's title can describe its general authorizing purpose without describing what a specific year's funding call actually prioritizes. Read the current year's own funding priority before assuming a promising-sounding name is a fit.

## About this date

Real 2026 deadline, shown for reference — this cycle's wood-utilization focus makes it an unlikely fit for food-forest planting itself.
