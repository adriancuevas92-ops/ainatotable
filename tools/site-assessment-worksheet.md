## Zone sketch

Attach or draw a rough map here. Mark:
- Where you already walk daily (this becomes Zone 1)

- Rough boundary of where the food forest will go

## What this tells you

Before moving on to [Design: The Layers & Guilds](../articles/03-design-layers-and-guilds.md), you should be able to answer:

- Where does the highest-maintenance, most-visited planting belong? (Zone 1)
- Where is erosion control a prerequisite before anything else goes in?
- Does this site actually support what you're hoping to grow, or does the plan need to change to fit the site?
