A quick-reference version of the species detail in [Plant Selection](../articles/04-plant-selection.md) — meant to be printed or kept open alongside the [Seven-Layer Planting Planner](seven-layer-planting-planner.md) while you design.

## Clear first, don't plant

If any of these are already established on your site, clearing is phase one, before any of the above goes in — see [Plant Selection](../articles/04-plant-selection.md) for why:

- Guinea grass
- Maile pilau
- Cat's claw
- Christmas berry

## Scope note

This list is deliberately limited to what's proven on a real wet-tropical Hawaiʻi site, not a comprehensive catalog of everything that can grow in the islands. It will grow as more of this venture's own planting produces real results worth adding.
