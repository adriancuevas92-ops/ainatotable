## Before you plant, check

- Does at least one plant in this guild fix nitrogen or accumulate nutrients for the others? If not, is that intentional?
- Have you checked mature size against actual spacing on your site map — not current nursery-pot size?
- Is anything in this guild an invasive species on your island, or does the site need clearing first? (See [Plant Selection](../articles/04-plant-selection.md) for the named invasives to clear before planting in Hawaiʻi.)
- Does the food-producing plant in this guild match what you actually want to harvest — for your own kitchen, a farmers market stand, or a restaurant relationship?
